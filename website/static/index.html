    From question to evidence

    A little more context
    for every answer.

    Pramaan uses your existing research pipeline to understand a question, find web sources, extract their text, rank useful passages and produce an answer.

    1. Ask in your languageEnglish, Hindi or Roman Hindi. Choose the answer language in the search box.
    2. Research across sourcesEvidence is collected and ranked for your question. Copied sources and poor OCR are treated cautiously.
    3. Inspect the answerClick a citation to inspect its source passage. Read the support explanation before relying on a result.

    Saved examples are labelled. Source extraction does not translate; Gemini synthesis requires backend configuration.
